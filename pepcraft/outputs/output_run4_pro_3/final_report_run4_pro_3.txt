### 4. Caveats and Recommendations
- **Database Matches vs. Functional Equivalence:** Sequence similarity to known AMPs (such as Cecropin-A Gm or Cathelicidin WAM2) indicates structural relatedness but does not guarantee identical functional potency or spectrum against *E. coli*.
- **Absence of Quantitative Efficacy Data:** Specific quantitative MIC values were not provided in the payload for the matched database entries; thus, comparative potency cannot be inferred directly from literature metrics without empirical assays.
- **D-Amino Acid Incorporation:** While D-amino acids generally improve proteolytic stability, they can also alter structural folding dynamics and peptide-membrane interactions, necessitating direct physical-chemical characterization.
- **Recommended Next Steps:** Prioritize the synthesis of these three candidates for in vitro broth microdilution assays against *E. coli*. Conduct circular dichroism (CD) spectroscopy to verify the target alpha-helical structural propensity in membrane-mimetic environments, alongside mammalian cytotoxicity (hemolysis) evaluations to assess therapeutic indices.
